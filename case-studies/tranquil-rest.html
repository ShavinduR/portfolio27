Riverside guest house — Full digital presence

Tranquil Rest.

A complete digital rebuild for a riverside guest house in Kosnathota: a new brand, every social channel, a booking website and a custom room management and billing system.

Client
Tranquil Rest, Kosnathota
Role
Rebrand, social media, website & PMS
Channels
All social channels (except YouTube) · Website
Lighthouse SEO score
100/100

01The problem

A guest house in a beautiful riverside setting, but with no consistent brand and no direct way for guests to discover it online and book without going through a middleman.

02What I did

  1. Rebranded the guest house: a river-inspired logo and mark, a green-and-yellow palette, and visiting cards with a dual Call / WhatsApp layout.
  2. Set up and ran its social media channels (everything except YouTube) with one consistent look and regular content.
  3. Designed and built a booking website with direct WhatsApp booking, so guests skip third-party booking fees.
  4. Optimised the site for search, reaching a perfect Lighthouse SEO score.

Beyond the brief: a room management & billing system

I also built a custom property management system (PMS) to handle rooms, bookings and billing. The same person who plans the posts can build the tools that run behind them.

03The result

The guest house now has one recognisable brand, from the visiting card to the booking page, and guests can go from a social post to a confirmed booking without a middleman.
