PM · PMR · ILM — Social media & content

Peak Moments Group.

Social media strategy and content production for a group of three photography brands, plus the three websites their content points to.

Client
Peak Moments, Peak Moments by Rumesh & I Like Media
Role
Social media strategy, content production & web
Timeframe
Dec 2025 — Aug 2026
Channels
Social media · 3 websites

01The problem

Three photography brands under one roof, each with a different audience: lifestyle shoots, a personal wedding brand and corporate media. Each one needed its own voice on social media, without the group looking like three unrelated businesses or the workload tripling.

02What I did

  1. Planned the social media strategy and content for all three brands, giving each its own audience, tone and content mix.
  2. Photographed and filmed boutique weddings and corporate events, then edited and colour-graded the work into posts and reels.
  3. Designed and built the three official websites, so social traffic lands on a page made to turn a visitor into a booking.
  4. Kept one visual language across the group, so every channel reads as the same studio.

03The result

All three brands now run an active social presence and a website of their own, with the strategy, the shoots, the edits and the sites handled by one person.
