Billiards venue — Social media, content & campaigns

Space Billiards.

Brand identity, social media content and campaigns for a billiards venue: promotional creatives, 9-ball match-highlight reels and giveaways.

Client
Space Billiards
Role
Brand, social media, content & campaigns
Channels
Social media

01The problem

A billiards venue that needed more than a page posting opening hours: a social presence that gave people a reason to follow, share and come in to play.

02What I did

  1. Designed the player-silhouette logo and carried it through every creative, so the venue is recognisable at a glance.
  2. Produced promotional creatives and brand visuals for a regular posting schedule.
  3. Filmed and edited 9-ball match-highlight reels that show the venue in action.
  4. Ran interactive giveaways to grow the audience and bring new players through the door.

03The result

The venue now posts with one consistent identity across promos, reels and giveaways, all of it planned, shot and designed by me.
